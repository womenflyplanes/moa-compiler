Mathematics of Arrays compiler

A universal intermediate representation for array languages is the research goal: portable, scalable, predictable and validated designs.

The completed attention correspondence covers 44 paired cases and 246 DNF components. The current checked path also has a bounded Fortran source adapter, structured versioned DNFs, operational forms, logical target lifting, and numerical/rejection checks. Supported generator paradigms include serial Fortran, OpenMP, OpenACC, MPI and MATLAB. Execution coverage is documented separately.
