\title{From $\psi$-Indexed Semantics to Machine-Shaped Execution: Mechanizing the Mathematics of Arrays}
\begin{abstract}
The Mathematics of Arrays (MoA) describes computations through array shapes and indexing, providing an algebraic route from semantic specifications to addressed and machine-shaped execution. We investigate how this route can be mechanized for forward attention, beginning with the indexed reductions underlying matrix multiplication. The intended compiler pipeline performs $\psi$-reduction to a machine-independent denotational normal form (DNF), lowers indexing through layout function $\gamma$ to operational normal form (ONF), and applies dimension lifting $\Omega$ to obtain legal machine-specific execution mappings. We distinguish mathematical proof obligations from numerical testing and hardware performance measurements. A restricted implementation consumes a saved typed forward-attention DNF/ONF trace and emits C for output-only attention using a stable online reduction that avoids materializing the attention-weight array. We give its inductive invariant and identify outstanding obligations for source-to-DNF normalization, machine-property-driven lifting, and comparison with hand-derived attention implementations. The methodology preserves unsuccessful prospective predictions alongside successes and separates historical hand-derived evidence from compiler-generated results.
\end{abstract}

Our objective is to derive implementations from one mathematical semantics across machine shapes, not simply to emit an attention kernel. Independently hand-derived and compiler-derived computations must agree at a canonical DNF under identical shape, domain, and observable-output contracts. Distinct legal derivation sequences are allowed. This paper addresses forward attention (Paper I) only.
\section{MoA Foundations}
For a shaped array $A$, $\rho A$ denotes its shape, $\langle i_0,\ldots,i_{r-1}\rangle\psi A$ selects a component, and $\tau(A)=\prod(\rho A)$. Matrix multiplication is expressed as
\[
\langle i,k\rangle\psi C=\mathop{+red}\nolimits_j((\langle i,j\rangle\psi A)\times(\langle j,k\rangle\psi B)).
\]
Contiguity is a property of the chosen layout and traversal, not an automatic consequence of the algebra.
\section{DNF, ONF, and $\Omega$}
DNF records the $\psi$-indexed computation independently of the target. Under contiguous rank-two layout,
\[
\gamma(\langle i,j\rangle,\langle n,d\rangle)=id+j.
\]
ONF derives addresses from indexed accesses. A legal $\Omega$ lifting assigns computation to a shaped machine while preserving observable semantics. Resource feasibility and communication obligations must be verified separately.
\subsection{A correctness obligation for addressing}
For an array with shape $\langle n,d\rangle$, assume $n,d>0$ and the valid Cartesian index domain $\iota\langle n,d\rangle$. The row-major map $\gamma(\langle i,j\rangle,\langle n,d\rangle)=id+j$ is injective: if $id+j=i'd+j'$ then $(i-i')d=j'-j$, and since $|j'-j|<d$, necessarily $i=i'$ and $j=j'$. It is surjective onto the $nd$ addresses by Euclidean division of any $0\le a<nd$ into $i=\lfloor a/d\rfloor$ and $j=a\bmod d$. Consequently $\gamma$ is a bijection between the valid index domain and the contiguous address domain, and $\langle i,j\rangle\psi A=(\operatorname{rav} A)[\gamma(\langle i,j\rangle,\rho A)]$ is a faithful indexed realization. This statement assumes the chosen row-major layout and correctly allocated storage; it does not prove arbitrary emitted instructions correct.

\section{Paper I Forward Attention}
Let $\rho Q=\langle n,d\rangle$, $\rho K=\langle m,d\rangle$, $\rho V=\langle m,d_v\rangle$, and $\rho O=\langle n,d_v\rangle$. Define
\[
s(i,k)=\frac{1}{\sqrt d}\mathop{+red}\nolimits_j((\langle i,j\rangle\psi Q)(\langle k,j\rangle\psi K)).
\]
With $a_i=\mathop{\mathrm{maxred}}\nolimits_k s(i,k)$, the output-only DNF contract is
\[
\langle i,v\rangle\psi O=
\frac{\mathop{+red}\nolimits_k(e^{s(i,k)-a_i}(\langle k,v\rangle\psi V))}
{\mathop{+red}\nolimits_k e^{s(i,k)-a_i}}.
\]
\section{Online Reduction: Inductive Invariant}
For a fixed query and prefix of $t$ keys, let
\[
a_t=\mathop{\mathrm{maxred}}\nolimits_{k<t}s(i,k),\quad z_t=\mathop{+red}\nolimits_{k<t}e^{s(i,k)-a_t},
\]
\[
\langle v\rangle\psi u_t=\mathop{+red}\nolimits_{k<t}(e^{s(i,k)-a_t}(\langle k,v\rangle\psi V)).
\]
Initially $a_0=-\infty$, $z_0=0$, and $u_0$ is the zero rank-one array with $\rho u_0=\langle d_v\rangle$. For new score $s$, let $a'=\max(a_t,s)$, $w_{\rm new}=e^{s-a'}$, and $w_{\rm old}=0$ for the empty prefix and $e^{a_t-a'}$ otherwise. Then
\[
z_{t+1}=w_{\rm old}z_t+w_{\rm new},
\]
\[
\langle v\rangle\psi u_{t+1}
=w_{\rm old}(\langle v\rangle\psi u_t)+w_{\rm new}(\langle t,v\rangle\psi V).
\]
The exponential identity $e^{x-a'}=e^{x-a_t}e^{a_t-a'}$ establishes the invariant by induction over finite keys. At $t=m$, $\langle i,v\rangle\psi O=(\langle v\rangle\psi u_m)/z_m$. This is exact-real reasoning; floating-point results require tolerance-based validation.
\subsection{A legal query-ownership lifting}
Let $P>0$ be a logical machine component count and $L=\lceil n/P\rceil$. Define a partition of the query index domain by
\[
I_p=\{pL+u\mid 0\le u<L,\;pL+u<n\},\quad 0\le p<P.
\]
For each $0\le i<n$, quotient and remainder with divisor $L$ give a unique $p=\lfloor i/L\rfloor$ and $u=i\bmod L$. Since $n\le PL$, $p<P$, proving that the $I_p$ cover all query indices exactly once. If each owner has access to all required key and value components, it can compute its assigned outputs without inter-owner reductions. This is a mathematical legality argument for query-disjoint lifting, not a demonstration that the present compiler selects $P$, allocates resources, or emits a parallel implementation from the ten-field machine description.

\section{Implementation and Evidence}
The restricted typed-trace emitter validates the saved DNF/ONF access schema and emits output-only C with $\gamma$-derived addresses. A test harness compiles generated C, checks finite results against an independent oracle, and rejects altered trace records. This does not yet demonstrate general source-to-DNF normalization or automated $\Omega$ lifting. Existing hand-derived ACCESS results are historical evidence, not compiler output.

\subsection{Provenance requirements}
A compiler-generated experimental record must bind the input expression, DNF, ONF, machine description, $\Omega$ ownership, generated source, predictions, and observations to immutable digests. The historical hand-derived path requires its own independent derivation and observable contract. Agreement of symbolic trace templates is insufficient to establish independent DNF convergence.

\section{Evaluation Protocol}
Predictions must be frozen before execution, with complete machine properties and SHA256-linked provenance. The final results will distinguish hand-derived from compiler-generated implementations and report unsuccessful holdouts alongside successful predictions. No unverified timing or universal library speedup is asserted.
\subsection{Prediction is a falsifiable claim}
A cost model must use the same target-machine shape and resource constraints that determine the generated mapping. For each candidate $\Omega$, the predicted cost is frozen before execution, and the selected mapping is compared with the measured fastest mapping. A wrong selection is retained as evidence rather than removed from the sample. Existing CPU OpenMP holdout records include a wrong mapping selection; the final manuscript must reproduce its exact provenance and conditions before reporting quantitative conclusions.

\section{Related Work and Limitations}
The final paper must incorporate checked citations to MoA publications, compiler transformations, polyhedral methods, array systems, and attention implementations. Source-to-DNF, machine lifting, hand-derived convergence, and reproducible hardware evaluation remain open.
\section{Conclusion}
MoA supplies a common language for semantics, addressing, and machine-shaped execution. The research contribution depends on mechanizing and verifying the entire transformation chain.
